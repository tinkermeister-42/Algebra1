3.2.1Two-Step Equations

Two operations means one new decision: which one do I undo first?
To simplify an expression you follow the order of operations. To solve an equation you go the opposite direction.
So undo addition and subtraction first, then multiplication and division.
EX 1. Two apples and one weight balance 7 weights. Find the weight a of one apple.
StepWhat I did to both sides
2a + 1 = 7the equation
2a + 1 − 1 = 7 − 1subtract 1 from both sides
2a = 6simplify
a = 3divide both sides by 2
EX 2. Solve 3x − 2 = 7.

3x − 2 + 2 = 7 + 2  →  3x = 9

3x ÷ 3 = 9 ÷ 3  →  x = 3

EX 3. Solve x8 − 3 = 7.
x8 − 3 = 7the equation
x8 = 10add 3 to both sides
x = 80multiply both sides by 8
EX 4. Solve x2 + 6 = 10.

x2 + 6 − 6 = 10 − 6  →  x2 = 4

x2 × 2 = 4 × 2  →  x = 8

EX 5. Solve −x + 5 = 3.

−x + 5 − 5 = 3 − 5  →  −x = −2

The coefficient is −1, so divide both sides by −1:

−x ÷ (−1) = −2 ÷ (−1)  →  x = 2

Remember: do not forget the −1
A variable with no number in front has a coefficient of 1. A variable with only a minus sign has a coefficient of −1.
EX 6. Solve 3 − x = 7.

3 − 3 − x = 7 − 3  →  −x = 4

−x ÷ (−1) = 4 ÷ (−1)  →  x = −4

3.2.2Word Problems with Two-Step Equations

EX 7. Babysitting. A sitter charges $10 for travel plus $8 per hour. The total was $42. How many hours?

Let h = the number of hours    Equation: 8h + 10 = 42

Answer: 4 hours

EX 8. Phone plan. $25 per month plus $0.05 per text. The bill was $32. How many texts?

Let t = the number of texts    Equation: 0.05t + 25 = 32

Answer: 140 texts

EX 9. Geometry. A rectangle has perimeter 28 cm. Its length is 2 more than twice its width. Find the width.

Let W = width, so L = 2W + 2

Perimeter formula: P = 2L + 2W

Substitute and simplify: 2(2W + 2) + 2W = 28  →  6W + 4 = 28

Answer: W = 4 cm

EX 10. Gym membership. $20 to join plus $15 per class. Your total was $65. How many classes?

Let c = the number of classes: 15c + 20 = 65

15c = 45  →  c = 3 classes
