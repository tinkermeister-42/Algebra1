3.2.1Two-Step Equations

When you simplify, follow PEMDAS. When you solve, go the other direction.
EX 1. Two apples and one weight balance 7 weights. Find the weight a of one apple.

2a + 1 − 1 = 7 − 1  →  2a = 6

2a ÷ 2 = 6 ÷ 2  →  a = 3

An apple weighs 3.

EX 2. Solve 7 = 3x − 2.your turn

7 + 2 = 3x − 2 + 2  →  9 = 3x

9 ÷ 3 = 3x ÷ 3  →  3 = x, so x = 3

EX 3. Solve x8 − 3 = 7.

x8 − 3 + 3 = 7 + 3  →  x8 = 10

x8 × 8 = 10 × 8  →  x = 80

EX 4. Solve 6 + x2 = 10.your turn

6 − 6 + x2 = 10 − 6  →  x2 = 4

x2 × 2 = 4 × 2  →  x = 8

EX 5. Solve −x + 5 = 3.

−x + 5 − 5 = 3 − 5  →  −x = −2

−x ÷ (−1) = −2 ÷ (−1)  →  x = 2

Remember: do not forget the −1
A variable with no number in front has a coefficient of 1. A variable with only a minus sign has a coefficient of −1.
EX 6. Solve 3 − x = 7.your turn

3 − 3 − x = 7 − 3  →  −x = 4

−x ÷ (−1) = 4 ÷ (−1)  →  x = −4

3.2.2Word Problems with Two-Step Equations

EX 7. Babysitting. A sitter charges $10 for travel plus $8 per hour. The total was $42. How many hours?

Let h = the number of hours.    Equation: 10 + 8h = 42

8h = 32  →  h = 4 hours

EX 8. Phone plan. $25 per month plus $0.05 per text. The bill was $32. How many texts?your turn

Let t = the number of texts.    Equation: 25 + 0.05t = 32

0.05t = 7  →  t = 140 texts

EX 9. Geometry. A rectangle has perimeter 28 cm. Its length is 2 more than twice its width. Find the width.

Let W = the width. The length is L = 2W + 2.

2W + 2L = 28  →  2W + 2(2W + 2) = 28  →  6W + 4 = 28

6W = 24  →  W = 4 cm

EX 10. Gym membership. $20 to join plus $15 per class. Your total was $65. How many classes?your turn

Let c = the number of classes: 20 + 15c = 65

15c = 45  →  c = 3 classes

Summary
adddividemultiplyotherreversesubtract1−1

1. To solve an equation, use the order of operations in reverse.

2. Whatever you do to one side of an equation, do to the other side.

3. In 6 + x2 = 10, first subtract 6 from both sides. Then multiply both sides by 2.

4. In −x + 5 = 3, the coefficient of x is −1.

19. A taxi charges $1.20 to start plus $0.50 per mile. Your bill was $6.20. How many miles?

Equation: 1.2 + 0.5m = 6.2

0.5m = 5  →  m = 10 miles

20. A theater charges $9 for admission and $4 per popcorn. Your total was $25. How many popcorns?

Equation: 9 + 4p = 25

4p = 16  →  p = 4 popcorns

21. Celsius is C = 59(F − 32). If C = 30, what is F?

30 = 59(F − 32)

30 × 95 = F − 32  →  54 = F − 32
