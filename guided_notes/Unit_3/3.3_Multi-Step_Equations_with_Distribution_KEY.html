3.3.1Distribution in Equations

Sometimes the variable is inside parentheses. Use the distributive property to simplify.

a(b + c) = ab + ac

EX 1. Solve 3(x + 4) = 21.

3x + 12 = 21  (distribute the 3)

3x + 12 − 12 = 21 − 12  →  3x = 9

3x ÷ 3 = 9 ÷ 3  →  x = 3

EX 2. Solve −2(2x − 5) = 6. Keep the negative with the 2.

−4x + 10 = 6  (distribute the −2)

−4x + 10 − 10 = 6 − 10  →  −4x = −4

−4x ÷ (−4) = −4 ÷ (−4)  →  x = 1

EX 3. Solve 3(2x + 1) = 15.your turn

6x + 3 = 15  →  6x = 12  →  x = 2

3.3.2Combining Like Terms in Equations

Sometimes there are like terms on the same side of the equals sign. Combine them into one term.
EX 4. Solve 4x + 7 + 2x = 31.

6x + 7 = 31  (combine like terms)

6x + 7 − 7 = 31 − 7  →  6x = 24

6x ÷ 6 = 24 ÷ 6  →  x = 4

EX 5. Solve 2(x + 5) + 3x = 25. Parentheses and like terms.

2x + 10 + 3x = 25  (distribute the 2)

5x + 10 = 25  (combine like terms)

5x + 10 − 10 = 25 − 10  →  5x = 15

5x ÷ 5 = 15 ÷ 5  →  x = 3

EX 6. Solve −3(x − 4) + 2x − 3 = 7. Careful with the signs.

−3x + 12 + 2x − 3 = 7  (distribute the −3)

−x + 9 = 7  (combine like terms)

−x + 9 − 9 = 7 − 9  →  −x = −2

−x ÷ (−1) = −2 ÷ (−1)  →  x = 2

EX 7. Solve 4(x + 2) − 3x + 5 = 18.your turn

4x + 8 − 3x + 5 = 18  →  x + 13 = 18

x + 13 − 13 = 18 − 13  →  x = 5

3.3.3Word Problems with Multi-Step Equations

EX 8. T-shirts. A club sells shirts for $12 each. Making them costs $60 plus $6 per shirt. Their profit was $84. How many shirts did they sell?

Let s be the number of shirts sold.   Profit = money in − money out.

Equation: 12s − (60 + 6s) = 84

12s − 60 − 6s = 84  →  6s − 60 = 84  →  6s = 144  →  s = 24

They sold 24 shirts.

EX 9. Geometry. A triangle has perimeter 54 cm. The sides are 2x + 3, 3x − 2, and x + 5. Find the value of x.

Equation: (2x + 3) + (3x − 2) + (x + 5) = 54

6x + 6 = 54  →  6x = 48  →  x = 8

EX 10. Car wash. A car wash charges $8 per car. Supplies cost $24 plus $2 per car. The group earned $96 in profit. How many cars did they wash?your turn

Let c be the number of cars: 8c − (24 + 2c) = 96

6c − 24 = 96  →  6c = 120  →  c = 20

They washed 20 cars.

Word Problems

21. A plumber charges $40 for the service call plus $25 per hour, and brought an assistant who also works at $15 per hour. The bill was $160. How many hours did they work?

Equation: 40 + 25h + 15h = 160

40h = 120, so 3 hours

22. A contractor charges $20 per foot of railing plus a $60 permit fee for a rectangular deck that is 3 feet longer than it is wide. The railing goes all the way around the deck. The total was $2420. Find the width.

Equation: 20(4w + 6) + 60 = 2420

Perimeter = 2w + 2(w + 3) = 4w + 6

80w + 180 = 2420  →  80w = 2240, so the width is 28 ft

23. A triangle has perimeter 75 cm with sides 3x + 1, 2(x + 4), and x + 6. Write and solve an equation for x.

Equation: (3x + 1) + 2(x + 4) + (x + 6) = 75

6x + 15 = 75  →  x = 10

Challenge

24. T-shirts cost $12 and hoodies cost $24. The number of hoodies ordered was 3 more than twice the number of T-shirts. The total was $312. How many T-shirts?

Let t = T-shirts, so hoodies = 3 + 2t.

12t + 24(3 + 2t) = 312  →  60t + 72 = 312

60t = 240, so 4 T-shirts

25. Solve 2(x + y) + 3x − 4 = 5y + 6 for x in terms of y.

2x + 2y + 3x − 4 = 5y + 6

5x = 3y + 10, so x = 3y + 105
