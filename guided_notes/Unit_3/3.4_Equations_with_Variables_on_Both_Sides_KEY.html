3.4  Equations With Variables on Both Sides

Guided Notes - Unit 3: Solving Linear Equations - DHS Algebra 1
Teacher Key: answers in red
Objectives
  • Solve equations with variables on both sides using inverse operations
  • Simplify with distribution or like terms before solving

Warm-Up

1. Solve 5x − 2 = 13

5x = 15, x = 3

2. Are 3(5x − 1) and 12x + 3(x − 1) equivalent?

Yes. Both simplify to 15x − 3.

3. Swanhilda solved 3(x − 7) = 3 like this. Did she do it correctly?

3x − 21 = 3  →  3x − 21 + 21 = 3  →  3x = 3  →  x = 1

No. She added 21 to the left only. It should be 3x = 24, so x = 8.

3.4.1The Basic Strategy

Now the variable can be on both sides. Get all the variables on one side and the numbers on the other.
EX 1. Three apples and 1 weight balance one apple and 7 weights. How much does an apple weigh?

3a + 1 − a = a + 7 − a  →  2a + 1 = 7

2a + 1 − 1 = 7 − 1  →  2a = 6

2a ÷ 2 = 6 ÷ 2  →  a = 3. An apple weighs 3.

Gotcha: do it to both sides

Taking an apple off only the right pan breaks the balance.

What you do to one side, you must do to the other side.

EX 2. Solve 4x + 5 = 2x + 9.

4x + 5 − 2x = 2x + 9 − 2x  →  2x + 5 = 9

2x + 5 − 5 = 9 − 5  →  2x = 4

2x ÷ 2 = 4 ÷ 2  →  x = 2

EX 3. Solve 5x + 3 = 2x + 12.your turn

5x + 3 − 2x = 2x + 12 − 2x  →  3x + 3 = 12

3x = 9  →  x = 3

3.4.2Simplifying Before Solving

Sometimes a side has parentheses or like terms. Simplify each side (distribute, combine like terms) before you solve.
EX 4. Solve 2(x + 3) = 3x + 1.

2x + 6 = 3x + 1  (distribute the 2)

2x + 6 − 3x = 3x + 1 − 3x  →  −x + 6 = 1

−x + 6 − 6 = 1 − 6  →  −x = −5

−x ÷ (−1) = −5 ÷ (−1)  →  x = 5

EX 5. Solve 4x + 6 + x = 2x + 12.

5x + 6 = 2x + 12  (combine like terms)

5x + 6 − 2x = 2x + 12 − 2x  →  3x + 6 = 12

3x + 6 − 6 = 12 − 6  →  3x = 6

3x ÷ 3 = 6 ÷ 3  →  x = 2

EX 6. Solve 3(x − 2) + 4 = 2(x + 1).

3x − 6 + 4 = 2x + 2  (distribute both sides)

3x − 2 = 2x + 2  (combine like terms)

3x − 2 − 2x = 2x + 2 − 2x  →  x − 2 = 2

x − 2 + 2 = 2 + 2  →  x = 4

EX 7. Solve 2(x + 4) = x + 10.your turn

2x + 8 = x + 10  (distribute the 2)

2x + 8 − x = x + 10 − x  →  x + 8 = 10

x + 8 − 8 = 10 − 8  →  x = 2

3.4.3Word Problems with Variables on Both Sides

Sometimes two different plans describe the same total. Then the equation has the variable on both sides.
EX 8. Two pay plans. Alex earns a $6 bonus plus $15 per hour. Blair earns an $18 bonus plus $12 per hour. After how many hours will they earn the same amount?

Let h be the number of hours.    Equation: 15h + 6 = 12h + 18

3h + 6 = 18  →  3h = 12  →  h = 4. They earn the same after 4 hours.

EX 9. Equal perimeters. Rectangle A has length x + 6 and width x + 2. Rectangle B has length 2x − 1 and width x + 3. They have the same perimeter. Find x.

Perimeter: P = 2(length + width)

Equation: 2((x + 6) + (x + 2)) = 2((2x − 1) + (x + 3))

2(2x + 8) = 2(3x + 2)  →  4x + 16 = 6x + 4

−2x + 16 = 4  →  −2x = −12  →  x = 6

EX 10. Two vendors. Vendor A sells sweatshirts for $18 each, includes 2 free, and charges $12 shipping. Vendor B sells them for $15 each with $42 flat shipping. For how many sweatshirts do the totals match?

Let s be the number of sweatshirts ordered.

Vendor A total: 18(s − 2) + 12    Vendor B total: 15s + 42

18s − 36 + 12 = 15s + 42  →  18s − 24 = 15s + 42

3s − 24 = 42  →  3s = 66  →  s = 22 sweatshirts

EX 11. Break-even. Plan A costs $8 per month plus $3 per premium movie. Plan B costs $2 per month plus $5 per premium movie. For how many movies will the plans cost the same in a month?your turn

Let m be the number of movies: 8 + 3m = 2 + 5m

8 − 2m = 2  →  −2m = −6  →  m = 3 movies

Summary
combinedistributeequalmultiplyothersame

1. Get the variables on one side and the constants on the other side.

2. In 2(x + 3) = 3x + 1, we distribute the 2 to simplify the left side.

3. When two plans cost the same, we set their totals equal.

Word Problems

21. Plan A charges $10 per month plus $4 per movie. Plan B charges $6 per month plus $5 per movie. After how many movies is the monthly cost the same?

Equation: 10 + 4m = 6 + 5m

m = 4 movies

22. Service A charges $25 per visit plus $15 per hour. Service B charges $40 per visit plus $10 per hour. After how many hours are the costs equal?

Equation: 25 + 15h = 40 + 10h

5h = 15, so 3 hours

23. Two sides of a trapezoid are equal. One is 4x + 3 cm and the other is 2(x + 6) cm. Find x.

4x + 3 = 2(x + 6) = 2x + 12

2x = 9, so x = 4.5

Challenge

24. Contractor A charges a $1200 setup fee plus $35 for every square meter beyond the base design. Contractor B charges $50 per square meter with a $300 discount. For what size are their prices the same?

1200 + 35s = 50s − 300

1500 = 15s, so s = 100 square meters

25. Solve 3(x + 2) + 4y = 2(x − y) + 10 for x in terms of y.

3x + 6 + 4y = 2x − 2y + 10

x = 4 − 6y
