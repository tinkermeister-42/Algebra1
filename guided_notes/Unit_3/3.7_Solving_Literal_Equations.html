3.7.1What Is a Literal Equation?

A literal equation is an equation made mostly of — a formula. Solving one means one variable in terms of the others, using the same as always.
EX 1. The area of a rectangle is A = lw. Solve for w.
A = lwthe formula
EX 2. d = rt. Solve for t.     EX 3. V = IR. Solve for I.

3.7.2With Addition or Subtraction

Move whole terms with addition or subtraction you multiply or divide.
EX 4. P = 2l + 2w. Solve for w.
P = 2l + 2wthe formula
EX 5. y = mx + b. Solve for x.
