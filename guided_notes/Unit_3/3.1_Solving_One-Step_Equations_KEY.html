3.1.1One-Step Equations: Addition and Subtraction

An equation is two expressions joined by an equal sign. Think of a balance scale: whatever you do to one side you must do to the other.
Our goal is to get the variable by itself, and we do it with inverse operations.
EX 1. A scale holds an apple plus 2 weights on the left and 5 weights on the right. Find the weight a of the apple.
StepWhat I did to both sides
a + 2 = 5the equation
a + 2 − 2 = 5 − 2subtract 2 from both sides
a = 3simplify

We undo addition by subtracting.

EX 2. Solve x + 9 = 11.

x + 9 − 9 = 11 − 9  →  x = 2

EX 3. Solve x − 5 = 8.
x − 5 = 8the equation
x − 5 + 5 = 8 + 5add 5 to both sides
x = 13simplify

We undo subtraction by adding.

EX 4. Solve x − 12 = 2.

x − 12 + 12 = 2 + 12  →  x = 14

3.1.2One-Step Equations: Multiplication and Division

EX 5. Two apples balance 6 weights. Find the weight a of one apple.
2a = 6the equation
2a ÷ 2 = 6 ÷ 2divide both sides by 2
a = 3simplify

2a means “multiply by 2”, so we undo it by dividing by 2.

EX 6. Solve 3x = 15.

3x ÷ 3 = 15 ÷ 3  →  x = 5

EX 7. Solve a3 = 1.
a3 = 1the equation
a3 × 3 = 1 × 3multiply both sides by 3
a = 3simplify

Dividing is undone by multiplying.

EX 8. Solve x5 = 10.

x5 × 5 = 10 × 5  →  x = 50

Remember — inverse operations
If you see…Undo with…Example

−add (+)x − 9 = 4
×divide (÷)4x = 20
÷multiply (×)x3 = 6
Do the same move to both sides, every time.

3.1.3Checking a Solution

To check, put your answer back into the original equation. If both sides come out equal, you are right.
EX 9. Solve 3x = 21 and check your answer.

Solve:   3x = 21 → x = 7

Check:   3(7) = 21 ✓

EX 10. Solve and check.   (a) 2y = 10    (b) z−3 = 5

(a) 2y = 10 → y = 5   check: 2(5) = 10 ✓

(b) z−3 = 5 → z = −15   check: −15−3 = 5 ✓

Why bother checking?
It catches mistakes, it confirms your answer really works, and on a test it can earn partial credit.
