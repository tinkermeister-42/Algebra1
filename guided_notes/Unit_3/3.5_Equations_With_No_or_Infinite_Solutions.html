3.5.1Equations With No Solution

Sometimes the variables cancel out. You are left with a statement that is false. Then no value makes both sides equal. The equation has no solution.
EX 1. Solve 3x + 5 = 3x − 2.

EX 2. Solve 2(x + 4) = 2x + 7.

Conclusion:

Remember: how to recognize no solution
You solve and get two different numbers on the two sides of the equals sign. Then there is no solution.

3.5.2Equations With Infinitely Many Solutions

Sometimes the variables cancel out and you are left with a statement that is always true. Then every number works. The equation has infinitely many solutions.
EX 3. Solve 4(x + 2) = 2(2x + 4).

Conclusion:

EX 4. Solve 5y − 3 + 2y = 7y − 3.

Conclusion:

Remember: how to recognize infinitely many solutions
You solve and get the same thing on each side of the equals sign. Then there are infinitely many solutions. All real numbers work.

Remember: how many solutions?
After you simplify each sideHow many solutions
Different number of x’sone solution
Same number of x’s, different numbersno solution (∅)
Both sides the sameinfinitely many solutions (ℝ)
∅ means “no solution”. ℝ means “all real numbers”.
