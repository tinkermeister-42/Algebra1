3. You have $50. After a $12 game, how many $8 accessories could you afford if you spent it all?

{{a}}

50 − 12 = 38, and 38 ÷ 8 = 4.75, so 4 accessories.

{{/a}}
Why we need inequalities
Bread costs $3.50 and candy is $1.25 a package. With $20, an equation gives c = 13.2 packages — but you cannot buy part of a package. Equations give one exact answer; real problems often have a {{a}}range{{/a}} of answers.
