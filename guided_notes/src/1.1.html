Lesson Summary

  1. Right is {{a}}positive{{/a}}, left is {{a}}negative{{/a}}.
  2. Opposites: same {{a}}distance{{/a}} from zero, {{a}}opposite{{/a}} sides.
  3. |x| = {{a}}distance from zero{{/a}}, and is never {{a}}negative{{/a}}.
  4. “Greater” = farther {{a}}right{{/a}}, not farther from zero.
  5. Adding a negative moves {{a}}left{{/a}}; subtracting a negative moves {{a}}right{{/a}}.
