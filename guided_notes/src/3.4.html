3.4.2Simplifying Before Solving

Remember - the order of attack
  1. Simplify each side ({{a}}distribute{{/a}} and {{a}}combine like terms{{/a}}).
  2. Move the {{a}}variable terms{{/a}} to one side.
  3. Move the {{a}}constants{{/a}} to the other side.
  4. Solve for the variable.
  5. {{a}}Check{{/a}} your solution.

3.4.3Word Problems

When two different plans describe the {{a}}same{{/a}} total, you get a variable on both sides. Write each side, set them {{a}}equal{{/a}}, and solve.
EX 8. Two pay plans. Alex earns a $6 bonus plus $15/hour. Blair earns an $18 bonus plus $12/hour. When do they earn the same?

Let h = {{a}}the number of hours worked{{/a}}    Equation: {{a}}6 + 15h = 18 + 12h{{/a}}

Answer: {{a}}after 4 hours{{/a}}

{{a}}

3h = 12, so h = 4

{{/a}}
EX 9. Equal perimeters. Rectangle A is (x + 6) by (x + 2). Rectangle B is (2x − 1) by (x + 3). They have the same perimeter. Find x.

Perimeter formula: P = {{a}}2l + 2w{{/a}}

Equation: {{a}}4x + 16 = 6x + 4{{/a}}

{{a}}

A: 2(x+6) + 2(x+2) = 4x + 16    B: 2(2x−1) + 2(x+3) = 6x + 4

12 = 2x, so x = 6

{{/a}}
EX 10. Two vendors. Vendor A: $18 per sweatshirt, 2 free, plus $12 shipping. Vendor B: $15 each plus $42 flat shipping. When do the totals match?

Vendor A total: {{a}}18(s − 2) + 12{{/a}}    Vendor B total: {{a}}15s + 42{{/a}}

Answer: {{a}}22 sweatshirts{{/a}}

{{a}}

{{/a}}
EX 11. Break-even. Plan A: $8 per month plus $3 per movie. Plan B: $2 per month plus $5 per movie. When do they cost the same?
{{a}}

8 + 3m = 2 + 5m  →  6 = 2m

m = 3 movies
