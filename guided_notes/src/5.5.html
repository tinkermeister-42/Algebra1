Warm-Up

5.5.1Factoring When a ≠ 1

Before, every trinomial started with x2, so the first terms of the factors were {{a}}x{{/a}} automatically. With ax2 + bx + c they are not.
The one change: instead of factors of c that add to b, look for factors of {{a}}a · c{{/a}} that add to {{a}}b{{/a}}.
Then fill in the outside of the model by taking the {{a}}GCF{{/a}} of each row and each column.

(px)2 − q2 = {{a}}(px − q)(px + q){{/a}}
