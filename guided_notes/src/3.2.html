3.2.1Two-Step Equations

Two operations means one new decision: which one do I undo first?
To simplify an expression you follow {{a}}the order of operations{{/a}}. To solve an equation you go the {{a}}opposite{{/a}} direction.
So undo {{a}}addition{{/a}} and {{a}}subtraction{{/a}} first, then {{a}}multiplication{{/a}} and {{a}}division{{/a}}.
EX 1. Two apples and one weight balance 7 weights. Find the weight a of one apple.
StepWhat I did to both sides
2a + 1 = 7the equation
{{a}}2a + 1 − 1 = 7 − 1{{/a}}{{a}}subtract 1 from both sides{{/a}}
{{a}}2a = 6{{/a}}{{a}}simplify{{/a}}
{{a}}a = 3{{/a}}{{a}}divide both sides by 2{{/a}}
EX 2. Solve 3x − 2 = 7.
{{a}}

3x − 2 + 2 = 7 + 2  →  3x = 9

3x ÷ 3 = 9 ÷ 3  →  x = 3

{{/a}}

EX 4. Solve {{f x/2}} + 6 = 10.
{{a}}

{{f x/2}} + 6 − 6 = 10 − 6  →  {{f x/2}} = 4

{{f x/2}} × 2 = 4 × 2  →  x = 8

{{/a}}
EX 5. Solve −x + 5 = 3.
{{a}}

−x + 5 − 5 = 3 − 5  →  −x = −2

The coefficient is −1, so divide both sides by −1:

−x ÷ (−1) = −2 ÷ (−1)  →  x = 2

{{/a}}
Remember — do not forget the −1
A variable with no number in front has a coefficient of {{a}}1{{/a}}. A variable with only a minus sign has a coefficient of {{a}}−1{{/a}}.
EX 6. Solve 3 − x = 7.
{{a}}

3 − 3 − x = 7 − 3  →  −x = 4

−x ÷ (−1) = 4 ÷ (−1)  →  x = −4

{{/a}}

EX 9. Geometry. A rectangle has perimeter 28 cm. Its length is 2 more than twice its width. Find the width.

Let W = width, so L = {{a}}2W + 2{{/a}}

Perimeter formula: {{a}}P = 2L + 2W{{/a}}

Substitute and simplify: {{a}}2(2W + 2) + 2W = 28  →  6W + 4 = 28{{/a}}

Answer: {{a}}W = 4 cm{{/a}}
