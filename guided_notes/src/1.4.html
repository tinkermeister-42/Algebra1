1.4.3Division & Reciprocals

The reciprocal of a fraction is the fraction {{a}}flipped upside down{{/a}}.

reciprocal of {{f 3/4}} = {{a}}{{f 4/3}}{{/a}}

reciprocal of 5 = {{a}}{{f 1/5}}{{/a}}

Remember — Keep, Change, Flip

1. Keep the {{a}}first{{/a}} fraction.

2. Change ÷ to {{a}}×{{/a}}.

3. Flip the {{a}}second{{/a}} fraction.
