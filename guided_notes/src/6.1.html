6.1.1Identifying Quadratic Functions

A thrown ball rises, reaches a highest point, and falls. Its path is a smooth U shape. A quadratic function can model a curve like this.
Linear functions have degree 1. Their graphs are straight lines. Quadratic functions have degree 2. Their graphs curve once.
From an equation: if the highest power of x is 2, the function is quadratic.

6.1.3Domain and Range

A parabola goes on forever left and right. So the domain is all real numbers, written (−∞, ∞).
The range is the set of possible y values. For vertex (h, k): opens upward → [k, ∞). Opens downward → (−∞, k].
EX 13. y = 0.5(x − 1)2 − 1 has vertex (1, −1) and opens upward.
