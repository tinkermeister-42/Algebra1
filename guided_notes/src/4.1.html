4.1.1The Coordinate Plane

The coordinate plane is two number lines that cross.
  • The x-axis runs left and right.
  • The y-axis runs up and down.
  • They meet at the origin, (0, 0).
  • Every point has an ordered pair (x, y).
  • The axes make four quadrants, I to IV. Start in the top right and go counterclockwise.
To plot a point, start at the origin.
  1. Move left or right for x.
  2. Then move up or down for y.
  3. Mark the spot.

That's the answer!
An equation with one variable has one answer. On a number line, it is one point. An equation with two variables has many answers. All the (x, y) pairs that make the equation true line up to form a straight line. That is why we graph the equation. The line shows all the solutions.
Remember
f(x) means the same thing as y. It is a different name for the output of a function. So f(x) = 2x + 1 could also be written as y = 2x + 1.
