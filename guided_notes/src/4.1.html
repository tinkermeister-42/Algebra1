4.1.1The Coordinate Plane

The coordinate plane is two number lines crossed together.
  • The x-axis runs {{a}}horizontally, left and right{{/a}}.
  • The y-axis runs {{a}}vertically, up and down{{/a}}.
  • They meet at the origin, the point ( {{a}}0{{/a}} , {{a}}0{{/a}} ).
  • A point's ordered pair is ( {{a}}x{{/a}} , {{a}}y{{/a}} ): first move {{a}}right or left{{/a}}, then move {{a}}up or down{{/a}}.
  • The axes make {{a}}4{{/a}} quadrants, numbered {{a}}counterclockwise{{/a}} starting in the top right.

4.1.2Function Rules and Tables of Values

Each input/output pair is a {{a}}point{{/a}} you can plot. Connect enough of them and you can see what the function {{a}}looks{{/a}} like.
f(x) means the same thing as {{a}}y{{/a}}. So f(x) = 2x + 1 could also be written {{a}}y = 2x + 1{{/a}}.
