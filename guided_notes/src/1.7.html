1.7.1Part, Whole, or Percent?

Every percent problem has three pieces. Know two, find the third.

part - {{a}}the piece you are looking at{{/a}}

whole - {{a}}the total amount{{/a}}

percent - {{a}}how many out of 100{{/a}}

part = {{a}}percent{{/a}} × {{a}}whole{{/a}}

Always convert the percent to a {{a}}decimal{{/a}} first.
Translating words: “is” becomes {{a}}={{/a}} and “of” becomes {{a}}×{{/a}}.

whatis15%of80?
{{a}}x{{/a}}{{a}}={{/a}}{{a}}0.15{{/a}}{{a}}×{{/a}}{{a}}80{{/a}}

Answer: {{a}}12{{/a}}

EX 2. What is 10% of 50?
{{a}}

0.10 × 50 = 5

EX 3. Finding the percent: Out of 50 students, 30 like soccer. What percent is that?

“Out of” tells you 50 is the {{a}}whole{{/a}} and 30 is the {{a}}part{{/a}}.

{{f part/whole}} = {{f ___/___}} = {{a}}0.6{{/a}} = {{a}}60%{{/a}}

{{a}}

{{f 30/50}} = 0.6

EX 5. Finding the whole: 25% of a number is 10. What is the number?

Way 1 - think in parts: 25% = {{f 1/4}}, so the whole is {{a}}4{{/a}} groups of 10 = {{a}}40{{/a}}

17. You have 88% of 325 points in Algebra. The last exam is worth 100 points. An A takes 90%. Is an A possible? If so, how many points do you need on the exam?
{{a}}
