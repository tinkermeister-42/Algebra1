2.1.1What Is a Variable?

A variable is a {{a}}letter or symbol{{/a}} that stands for a number that is {{a}}unknown{{/a}} or that can {{a}}change{{/a}}.
Think of it as the label on a sealed box - you know a number is inside, you just do not know which one yet.
EX 1. A sealed box of apples gets the label x. You open it and count 3 apples. What changed?

Before opening, x was a {{a}}variable - an unknown number{{/a}}.

After opening, you know x = {{a}}3{{/a}}.

2.1.3Terms, Coefficients, and Constants

  • Terms are separated by {{a}}+{{/a}} or {{a}}−{{/a}}.
  • A coefficient is the number that {{a}}multiplies{{/a}} a variable.
  • A constant is a number {{a}}without{{/a}} a variable.
EX 5. Break apart 4x2 + 2y − 7.
Terms{{a}}4x2,  2y,  −7{{/a}}

The + or − in front {{a}}belongs{{/a}} to the term. A lone variable has an implied coefficient of {{a}}1{{/a}}.
