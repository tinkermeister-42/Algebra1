1.7.1Part, Whole, or Percent?

Every percent problem has three pieces. Know two, find the third.

part - the piece you are looking at

whole - the total amount

percent - how many out of 100

part = percent × whole

Always convert the percent to a decimal first.
Translating words: “is” becomes = and “of” becomes ×.
EX 1. Finding the part: What is 15% of 80?
whatis15%of80?
x=0.15×80

Answer: 12

EX 2. What is 10% of 50?

0.10 × 50 = 5

EX 3. Finding the percent: Out of 50 students, 30 like soccer. What percent is that?

“Out of” tells you 50 is the whole and 30 is the part.

partwhole = ______ = 0.6 = 60%

3050 = 0.6

EX 4. If 3 out of 75 students get enough sleep, what percent is that?

375 = 0.04 = 4%

EX 5. Finding the whole: 25% of a number is 10. What is the number?

Way 1 - think in parts: 25% = 14, so the whole is 4 groups of 10 = 40

Way 2 - divide: 10 ÷ 0.25 = 40

EX 6. 20% of a number is 12. What is the number?

12 ÷ 0.20 = 60

1.7.2Real-Life Examples

EX 7. Grades. You got 19 out of 20 points. What is your grade?

1920 = 0.95 = 95%    Letter grade: A

EX 8. John got 20 out of 25 questions correct. What is his grade?

2025 = 0.8 = 80% - a B

EX 9. Sales. A $60 jacket is 25% off. What is the new price?

Step 1 - find the discount: 0.25 × 60 = $15

Step 2 - subtract it from the original price: 60 − 15 = 45

New price: $45

EX 10. Swanhilda finds a $300 prom dress at 65% off. What does it cost?

Discount: 0.65 × 300 = $195, so the dress costs 300 − 195 = $105 (or 35% of 300 straight away).

EX 11. Surveys. 75% of teachers surveyed prefer cookies. If 120 preferred cookies, how many teachers were surveyed?

Are you looking for the part, the whole, or the percent? the whole

Work: 120 ÷ 0.75    Answer: 160 teachers

Tip - check yourself
Before you compute, ask: what answer do I expect? Then check that your answer is close to it.
EX 12. A survey found 550 students - which is 55% - like cucumbers. How many students were surveyed?

550 ÷ 0.55 = 1000 students

Real-Life Scenarios

9. A driver's test has 30 questions. You need at least 24 points to pass. What percent do you need?

Answer: 80%

10. A student scores 27 out of 30 on one test and 42 out of 50 on another. What percent of the total points did they earn?

Answer: 86.25%

27 + 4230 + 50 = 6980 = 0.8625

11. A backpack is 25% off. It was $80. Find the discount and the new price.

Discount: $20    New price: $60

12. Happy hour takes 30% off drinks. A soda usually costs $3.50. Find the discount and the happy hour price.

Discount: $1.05    Price: $2.45

13. A survey shows 68% of people prefer cats to dogs. If 250 people were asked, how many chose dogs?

Answer: 80 people

100% − 68% = 32% chose dogs, and 0.32 × 250 = 80

14. In a quality test, 3% of fidget spinners were defective. If 60 were defective, how many were tested?

Answer: 2000 spinners

60 ÷ 0.03 = 2000

Challenge

15. A class has 24 students. 13 are in choir and 25% are in band. The rest are in art. What percent are in art?

Choir 13 × 24 = 8, band 0.25 × 24 = 6, so art gets 24 − 8 − 6 = 10 students.

1024 ≈ 41.7%

16. A shirt is marked down 40% and now costs $27. What was the original price?

$27 is 60% of the original: 27 ÷ 0.60 = $45

17. You have 88% of 325 points in Algebra. The last exam is worth 100 points. An A takes 90%. Is an A possible? If so, how many points do you need on the exam?

Points so far: 0.88 × 325 = 286. After the exam the total is 425.

An A needs 0.90 × 425 = 382.5, so you need 382.5 − 286 = 96.5 → 97 points. Yes, an A is still possible.
