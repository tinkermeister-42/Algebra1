Warm-Up

1. You simplify 10 − 4 × 2 and get 2. Your friend gets 12. Who is right?

You are. 10 − 4 × 2 = 10 − 8 = 2. Your friend worked left to right instead of multiplying first.

2. This one went viral - 9 out of 10 people miss it.   6 ÷ 2(1 + 2)

6 ÷ 2(1 + 2) = 6 ÷ 2 × 3 = 3 × 3 = 9 - ÷ and × are done left to right.

1.8.1The Order Matters

EX 1. Simplify 10 + 3 × 5 two ways. Which one is correct?

Left to right: 13 × 5 = 65

Multiplication first: 10 + 15 = 25

Correct answer: 25    Why? PEMDAS says multiply before you add

Remember: PEMDAS
PParentheseswork inside grouping symbols first
EExponentspowers and roots
M / DMultiply and Divideleft to right
A / SAdd and Subtractleft to right
M and D are on the same level, so do them left to right. Same for A and S.
Why are × and ÷ grouped together?
Because they are two sides of the same operation. Dividing by a number is the same as multiplying by its reciprocal: 12 ÷ 32 = 12 × ______ = 8
In the same way, subtracting is adding the opposite: 5 − 2 = 5 + (−2)

1.8.2Examples with Integers

EX 2. 5 + 3 × 2      EX 3. (5 + 3) × 2      EX 4. 8 − 12 ÷ 3

5 + 3 × 2 = 5 + 6 = 11

(5 + 3) × 2 = 8 × 2 = 16

8 − 12 ÷ 3 = 8 − 4 = 4

What single change made EX 2 and EX 3 different? the parentheses. They force the addition to happen first

Nested Parentheses

Work from the inside out.
What counts as grouping? Parentheses ( ), brackets [ ], braces { }, and the one that is easy to forget, the fraction bar.
Brackets and braces are parentheses in a different shape; they keep the layers clear when grouping is nested.

1.8.5Why This Matters

EX 12. You have a 25%-off coupon and a $10 gift card. The item costs $40. Which order is better for you?

Coupon first: 40 × 0.75 = 30, then 30 − 10 = $20

Gift card first: 40 − 10 = 30, then 30 × 0.75 = $22.50

Better deal: coupon first, at $20. Order matters!
