1. Monique ate 1 slice of pepperoni and 2 slices of cheese. Both pizzas were cut into 8 equal slices. How much pizza did she eat?

Answer: 38

2. Jack ate 1 slice of a pepperoni pizza cut into 3 pieces and 1 slice of a cheese pizza cut into 4 pieces. How much pizza did he eat?

Answer: 712

1.5.1Like Denominators

Monique ate 1 slice + 2 slices, and both pizzas were cut into 8. Every slice is the same size, so just count slices: 18 + 28 = 38.
The denominator says how big each piece is. The pieces never changed size, so it stays. The numerator counts how many pieces you have. That is the part that changes.
  • Keep the denominator.
  • Combine the numerators.
  • Always check whether you can simplify.
Gotcha: numerators only
Do not combine the denominators. 27 + 37 is not 514.

1.5.2Unlike Denominators

EX 5. 13 + 14 (Jack's pizza).  The thirds and the fourths are not the same size, so cut both into twelfths.

44 × 13 + 14 × 33 = ___12 + ___12

Answer: 412 + 312 = 712

Gotcha: top and bottom
44 = 1, so multiplying by it does not change the value. Multiply only the bottom and you have changed the fraction into a different number.
Different denominators mean the pieces are different sizes. A common denominator is a number that both denominators divide into evenly.
To find one: list the multiples of each and take the first number both lists show.

Multiples of 3: 3, 6, 9, 12, 15…

Multiples of 4: 4, 8, 12, 16…  →  common denominator: 12

Then divide to find what each fraction gets multiplied by: 12 ÷ 3 = 4 and 12 ÷ 4 = 3.
EX 6. 78 − 14

Multiples of 8: 8, 16, 24…    Multiples of 4: 4, 8, 12…

Common denominator: 8    8 ÷ 4 = 2    the first fraction is already in eighths.

78 − 14 × 22 = 78 − 28 = 58

If you are ok having to do more work simplifying your answer, you can skip writing out multiples by multiplying the two denominators together instead. For 56 + 79 that gives 54 instead of 18. Same answer, but bigger numbers to simplify at the end.

1.5.3Adding & Subtracting Mixed Numbers

Why not combine the whole numbers, then the fractions? Try that on 614 − 338. You cannot take 38 away from 14, so you would have to borrow from the 6. Turn each mixed number into an improper fraction first instead.
  1. Convert each to an improper fraction: 3 × 4 + 1 = 13, so 314 = 134.
  2. Get a common denominator, then combine.
  3. Simplify. Convert back to a mixed number if you need to.
A whole number is a fraction over 1, so 8 = 81.
