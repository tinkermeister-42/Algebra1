5.1.1Understanding Powers

The base tells what number or variable is being multiplied. The exponent tells how many times to multiply that base by itself.
53 = 5 · 5 · 5 = 125. The base is 5. The exponent is 3.
Two powers with the same base (like x2 and x5) have like bases.
Rule 1: Product Rule. x3 · x5 = (x·x·x) · (x·x·x·x·x) = x8. This is x times itself 8 times.    In general: am · an = am+n

Common mistakes to avoid
  • (−3)2 = 9, but −32 = −9. The negative must be inside parentheses to be part of the base.
  • Exponents distribute over multiplication and division, not over addition and subtraction: (x + y)2 ≠ x2 + y2.
  • A negative exponent does not make the number negative. It moves the base to the other side of the fraction bar.
  • a0 = 1, not 0.
