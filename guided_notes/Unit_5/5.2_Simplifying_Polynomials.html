5.2.1Understanding Polynomials

A polynomial is a sum or difference of terms. Each term has a coefficient (a number), a variable, and a whole number exponent. The constant 5 fits too, because 5 = 5x0.
Polynomials:  3x2 + 2x − 5,   7y3 − 4y,   9
Not polynomials:  2x and x−3 + 4 (negative exponent),  √x + 5 (fractional exponent)
1 term: monomial (7x3).   2 terms: binomial (4x + 1).   3 terms: trinomial (x2 + 3x + 2).   More than 3 terms: no special name.
The degree of a polynomial is the largest exponent in it.

1. Distribute if parentheses are present.

2. Apply exponent rules when needed.

3. Combine like terms.

4. Rewrite in standard form.
