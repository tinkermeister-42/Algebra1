Warm-Up

1. A shirt costs $10. How do you find the cost of several shirts?

Multiply $10 by the number of shirts — 10s

2. You have some cookies and eat 3. How can you show how many are left?

c − 3, where c is how many you started with

3. A car drives 50 miles every hour. How do you find the distance for many hours?

50h, where h is the number of hours

2.1.1What Is a Variable?

A variable is a letter or symbol that stands for a number that is unknown or that can change.
Think of it as the label on a sealed box — you know a number is inside, you just do not know which one yet.
EX 1. A sealed box of apples gets the label x. You open it and count 3 apples. What changed?

Before opening, x was a variable — an unknown number.

After opening, you know x = 3.

2.1.2What Is an Expression?

An expression is a math phrase made of numbers, variables, and operations. Expressions do not compare values — so no = and no < or >.
EX 2. Circle the expressions. Cross out the ones that are not.

What makes the crossed-out ones different? they use = or an inequality sign, so they compare two things

EX 3. Which of these can be written as a single expression?
(a) the total pencils in 7 boxes    (b) whether the total pencils equals 20

(a) 7p — an expression

(b) 7p = 20 — an equation, not an expression

EX 4. You have 5 boxes and each holds x apples. Write an expression for the total. Then do 10 boxes of pencils.

5 boxes: 5x     10 boxes of pencils (let p = pencils per box): 10p

Gotcha — where did the multiplication sign go?
Three ways to write “3 times x”: 3x, 3 · x, 3(x).
We avoid × because it looks like the variable x.

2.1.3Terms, Coefficients, and Constants

  • Terms are separated by + or −.
  • A coefficient is the number that multiplies a variable.
  • A constant is a number without a variable.
EX 5. Break apart 4x2 + 2y − 7.
Terms4x2,  2y,  −7
Coefficients4 and 2
Constant−7
Gotcha — do not forget the sign
The + or − in front belongs to the term. A lone variable has an implied coefficient of 1.
EX 6. List the terms and coefficients.

−5x2 − 2x  →  terms: −5x2, −2x   coefficients: −5, −2

3x2 + x − y  →  terms: 3x2, x, −y   coefficients: 3, 1, −1

2.1.4Translating Between Words and Expressions

EX 8. Write an expression for each situation.

5 boxes of apples, x apples each: 5x

$12 per hour for h hours: 12h

3 more games than your friend, who has n: n + 3

A taxi charges $10 plus $2 per mile m: 2m + 10

A plant starts at g inches and grows 8 inches: g + 8

EX 9. A rectangle has length L. Its width is 2 more than 4 times the length. Write an expression for the width W.

W = 4L + 2
