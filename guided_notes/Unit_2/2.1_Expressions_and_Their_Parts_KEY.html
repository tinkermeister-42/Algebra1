Guided Notes - Unit 2: Algebraic Expressions - DHS Algebra 1
Teacher Key: answers in red
Objectives
  • Identify the parts of an algebraic expression
  • Describe what a variable, constant, coefficient, and term are

Warm-Up

1. A shirt costs $10. How do you find the cost of several shirts?

Multiply $10 by the number of shirts: 10s

2. You have some cookies and eat 3. How can you show how many are left?

c − 3, where c is how many you started with

3. A car drives 50 miles every hour. How do you find the distance for many hours?

50h, where h is the number of hours

2.1.1What Is a Variable?

A variable is a letter or symbol that stands for an unknown or changing number. Think of x as the label on a mystery box.
EX 1. Boxes of apples. You cannot see inside a sealed box, so you label it x. Then you open it and count the apples.

Before you open it, x is a variable. After you look, x = 3

2.1.2What Is an Expression?

An expression is a math phrase made of numbers, variables, and operations. Expressions do not compare values.
Expressions:  3x,   2x − 7,   12(y + 4),   x2 − 3x + 4,   9
Not expressions:  3x + 5 = 20 (an equation),   x > 7 (a comparison)
EX 2. Situation 1: the total number of pencils in 7 boxes. Situation 2: whether the total number of pencils is equal to 20. Can each one be shown with a single expression? Explain.your turn

Situation 1: Yes: 7p, if p is pencils per box

Situation 2: No: it is a comparison, not a single expression

EX 3. You have 5 boxes. Each box has x apples. Write an expression for the total.

Total apples: 5 · x or 5x

EX 4. Write an expression for 10 boxes of pencils. Let p = pencils per box.your turn

Total pencils: 10p

Gotcha: where did the multiply sign go?
Three ways to write “3 times x”:  3 × x (rarely used),  3 · x,  3x. We avoid × so it does not look like the variable x.

2.1.3Terms, Coefficients, and Constants

  • Terms are separated by + or −.
  • Coefficients multiply a variable (the number in front).
  • Constants are numbers without a variable.
EX 5. Break apart 4x2 + 2y − 7.
Terms4x2,  2y,  −7
Coefficients4 and 2
Constant−7
Gotcha: do not forget the sign
The sign (+ or −) belongs to the term. A lone variable has an implied coefficient of 1.
EX 6. List the terms and coefficients.

−5x2 − 2x  →  terms: −5x2, −2x   coefficients: −5, −2

3x2 + x − y  →  terms: 3x2, x, −y   coefficients: 3, 1, −1

EX 7. Make your own expression with 4 or more terms. List the parts.your turn

My expression: answers vary, for example 18x − 2y + 3z − 1

Terms: 18x, −2y, 3z, −1

Coefficients: 18, −2, 3    Constant: −1

2.1.4Translating Between Words and Expressions

EX 8. Write an expression for each situation.

5 boxes of apples, x apples in each box: 5x

You earn $12 per hour for h hours: 12h

Your friend has n games. You have 3 more than your friend: n + 3

A taxi charges $10 plus $2 for each mile m: 10 + 2m

A plant starts at g inches tall and grows 8 inches: g + 8

EX 9. A rectangle has length L. The width is 2 more than 4 times the length. Find an expression for the width W.your turn

W = 4L + 2

Summary
coefficientcompareconstantequationparenthesestermvariable1−1

1. A variable stands for an unknown or changing number.

2. An expression does not compare values.

3. In 4x2 + 2y − 7, the 4 is a coefficient.
The −7 is a number without a variable, so it is a constant.

4. In 3x2 + x − y, the coefficient of y is −1.

5. “Add 3 to x, then double” is 2(x + 3). The parentheses show that the adding comes first.
