2.3.1What Are Like Terms?

A messy pile of fruit is hard to count. We group it by type. Expressions can be messy in the same way.
Two terms are like terms if their variable part (letters and exponents) matches exactly.
Like terms:  5x and 3x;   7 and −3;   2y3 and y3;   8xy and 2yx (the order of the variables does not matter)
Not like terms:  2x and 3y;   4x2 and 2x4;   7 and −3x;   3x2y and 3xy2
The sign (+ or −) belongs with the term. In 7x − 2x the terms are +7x and −2x.
Gotcha: powers matter
p3 and p2 are not like terms. They have the same variable, but not the same exponents.
