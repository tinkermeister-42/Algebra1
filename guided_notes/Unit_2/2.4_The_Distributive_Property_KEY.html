2.4.1What Is the Distributive Property?

To distribute is to hand the multiplier to every term inside the parentheses.

a(b + c) = ab + ac   and   a(b − c) = ab − ac

EX 1. Expand 3(x + 2). Draw the arrows from the 3 to each term.

3( x   +   2 )  =  3x + 6

With algebra tiles: 3 groups of (x + 2) gives 3 x-tiles and 6 unit tiles.

Gotcha - do not skip a term
Multiply every term inside.   4(x + 2y + 5) = 4x + 8y + 20
EX 2. Expand 5(x + 7).

5x + 35

2.4.2Distributing with Negatives

EX 3. Expand −3(x − 4).

−3x + 12

EX 4. Expand −(2x − 7). What number is hiding in front of the parentheses?

A lone negative sign means multiply by −1.

−1(2x − 7) = −2x + 7

EX 5. Expand −2(3 − y).

−6 + 2y   (or 2y − 6)

2.4.3Distribute, Then Combine Like Terms

EX 6. Simplify 4(x + 2) + 3(x − 5).
Distribute4x + 8 + 3x − 15
Combine like terms7x − 7
Tip - make it simpler first
If you can simplify inside the parentheses first, do that before you distribute.   2(4x + 3 − 2x − 1) = 2(2x + 2) = 4x + 4
Gotcha - the sign travels with the multiplier
In 3(x − 2) − 5(4x + 5) you must multiply the second group by −5, not 5.
Result: 3x − 6 − 20x − 25 = −17x − 31

2.4.4Reverse It: Factoring Out a Common Factor

If ab + ac = a(b + c), then we can factor out the common factor (the GCF). This is the distributive property in reverse.
EX 7. Factor out the GCF.

8x + 12 = 4(2x + 3)

15x − 9 = 3(5x − 3)

3x + 6y = 3(x + 2y)

12x + 18 = 6(2x + 3)

2.4.5Check Your Work

EX 8. Spot the mistake. Paxton simplified 5(2x − 3) − (x − 4) like this:
Step 1: 10x − 3 − x − 4    Step 2: 9x − 7

Test the original at x = 3:   5(3) − (−1) = 15 + 1 = 16

Test his answer at x = 3:   9(3) − 7 = 20

Did he make a mistake? yes   What was it? he never multiplied the 5 by −3, and he did not distribute the − across (x − 4)

Correct answer: 9x − 11

Challenge

23. Simplify 4(2x + 3) − (2x + 3) two ways: distribute first, then factor out the common (2x + 3) first. Why do both give the same result?

Distribute first:   8x + 12 − 2x − 3 = 6x + 9

Factor first:   (2x + 3)(4 − 1) = 3(2x + 3) = 6x + 9

Both work because factoring is just the distributive property read backwards.

24. Use the distributive property to multiply (3x − 1)(2x + 3). Show each step.

(3x − 1)(2x + 3) = 3x(2x + 3) − 1(2x + 3)

= 6x2 + 9x − 2x − 3 = 6x2 + 7x − 3
