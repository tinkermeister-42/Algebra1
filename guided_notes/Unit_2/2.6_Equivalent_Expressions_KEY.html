2.6.1What Does “Equivalent” Mean?

Two expressions are equivalent if they give the same value for every input. They do not have to look the same.
EX 1. Compare A(x) = 3(x + 4) and B(x) = 3x + 12.
x3(x + 4)3x + 12
01212
52727

The outputs match. This is evidence that they are equivalent.

Remember - evidence is not proof
Testing a few values gives evidence. A proof shows it is true for all inputs.
EX 2. Are C(x) = 4x + 1 and D(x) = 4(x + 1) equivalent? Try x = 2.

C(2) = 8 + 1 = 9

D(2) = 4(3) = 12

A single value that makes the outputs differ is called a counterexample.

EX 4. Pick two easy inputs for each pair. Write evidence or counterexample.

a.  x + x + x and 3x   - evidence (equivalent)

b.  5(x − 2) and 5x − 10   - evidence (equivalent)

c.  2x + 3 and 2(x + 3)   - counterexample at x = 0: 3 vs 6

Think about it
Can testing values prove two expressions are not equivalent? yes - because one counterexample is enough to disprove it

2.6.2Proving Equivalence

We cannot test every value, so to prove equivalence we use the properties of algebra to rewrite each expression until they match.
The tools: the distributive property, combining like terms, and factoring.
EX 5. Distribute. Prove 2(3 − y) and 6 − 2y are equivalent.

2(3 − y) = 6 − 2y ✓

EX 6. Combine like terms. Prove 2x − 3y + 7 and 2 − 3x + 5 − 3y + 5x are equivalent.

2 − 3x + 5 − 3y + 5x

= (−3x + 5x) − 3y + (2 + 5) = 2x − 3y + 7 ✓

EX 7. Simplify both sides. Prove 2(x − 1) + 3x and 2x + 3(x − 2) + 4 are equivalent.

2(x − 1) + 3x = 2x − 2 + 3x = 5x − 2

2x + 3(x − 2) + 4 = 2x + 3x − 6 + 4 = 5x − 2

EX 8. Factor. Prove 6z − 2 and 2(3z − 1) are equivalent.

The GCF of 6z and −2 is 2.

6z − 2 = 2(3z − 1) ✓

EX 9. Disprove. Show 2x + 3 and 2(x + 3) are not equivalent, using algebra.

2(x + 3) = 2x + 6, and 2x + 6 ≠ 2x + 3 - the constants differ by 3 for every x.

EX 10. Prove 3 − 2(x + 1) and x + 1 − 3x are equivalent.

3 − 2(x + 1) = 3 − 2x − 2 = 1 − 2x

x + 1 − 3x = −2x + 1

Challenge

24. Find k so that 3(x + k) is equivalent to 3x + 12.

3(x + k) = 3x + 3k, so 3k = 12 and k = 4.

25. Are these equivalent for all x? Prove it or give a counterexample.
E1(x) = (x + 2)(4x − 1) + 3(x + 2)    E2(x) = 2(x + 2)(2x + 1)

Factor (x + 2) out of E1:

E1 = (x + 2)[(4x − 1) + 3] = (x + 2)(4x + 2) = 2(x + 2)(2x + 1)

That is exactly E2 - equivalent for all x.
