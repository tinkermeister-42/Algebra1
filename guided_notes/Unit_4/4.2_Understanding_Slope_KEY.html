Warm-Up

1. Plot f(x) = 2x + 1.    2. On the same grid plot g(x) = −3x + 1.    3. How are the lines alike? How are they different?

Alike:

Both are straight lines and both cross the y-axis at (0, 1).

Different:

f rises (slope 2); g falls and is steeper (slope −3).

4.2.1What Is Slope?

Picture climbing a stairway. Each step moves you forward and also up or down. Comparing those two amounts tells you how steep the stairs are. That is slope.

m = riserun = ΔyΔx

Δ (delta) means change in. So Δy is the change in y (the rise) and Δx is the change in x (the run).

If y goes up as x goes up, the slope is positive. If y goes down, the slope is negative. A bigger change in y makes the line steeper.

4.2.3The Slope Formula

When counting boxes is impractical, use the formula. For (x1, y1) and (x2, y2):

m = ΔyΔx = y2 − y1x2 − x1

EX 5. Find the slope through (−2, 8) and (10, 6).

x1 = −2, y1 = 8, x2 = 10, y2 = 6

m = 6 − 810 − (−2) = −212 = −16

EX 6. Find the slope through (3, −2) and (5, −5).

m = −5 − (−2)5 − 3 = −32 = −32

EX 7. Find the slope through (4, 3) and (2, 3).

m = 0    That means the line is horizontal, because the y-values never change.

EX 8. Find the slope through (5, −3) and (5, 6).

m = undefined    The slope is undefined because you cannot divide by zero. The line is vertical.

Straight up or straight down?
Someone at the bottom of a cliff says it goes straight up. Someone at the top says straight down. It cannot be both, so we call a vertical slope undefined.
EX 9. Find the slope between (0, 95) and (−50, 2).

m = 2 − 95−50 − 0 = −93−50 = 9350 = 1.86

Challenge

24. Two lines have slopes m1 = 3 and m2 = −13. What is true about how these lines relate to each other?

3 and −13 are negative reciprocals. Their product is −1, so the lines are perpendicular.

25. A line passes through the origin and a point (x, y). Show that if the slope doubles while x stays fixed, the new point satisfies y′ = 2y. What does that say about steepness?

Through the origin the slope is m = yx.

Doubling it: y′x = 2 · yx, and multiplying both sides by x gives y′ = 2y.

So at the same run the line rises twice as far. That is twice as steep.
