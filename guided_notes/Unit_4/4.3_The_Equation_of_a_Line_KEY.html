4.3.1Graphing from a Point and the Slope

A line's slope never changes. So if you know one point and the slope, you can find as many other points as you want.
EX 1. Graph the line with slope m = 13 through (0, 1).

The slope means “up 1 for every 3 to the right.”

Starting at (0, 1), the next point is ( 3 , 2 ).

EX 2. Graph the line with slope m = 32 through (2, 2).

Up 3, right 2.

4.3.3Finding Intercepts Algebraically

Remember
Set y = 0 → you get the x-intercept.    Set x = 0 → you get the y-intercept.
EX 4. Find both intercepts of y = x − 5.

x-intercept (set y = 0):   0 = x − 5, so (5, 0)

y-intercept (set x = 0):   y = −5, so (0, −5)

EX 5. Find both intercepts of y = 2x + 1.

x-int: 0 = 2x + 1 → x = −12, so (−12, 0)

y-int: x = 0 → y = 1, so (0, 1)

4.3.4The Equation of a Line

Every non-vertical line can be written in slope-intercept form:

y = mx + b

m is the slope

b is the y-intercept

EX 6. Graph y = 23x − 2.

Start at the y-intercept: ( 0 , −2 )

Then use the slope: up 2, right 3.

EX 7. Graph y = −4x + 3.

Writing the slope as a fraction helps: m = −4 = −41

So from (0, 3) go down 4 and right 1.

EX 8. Graph f(x) = 3x − 2.

m = 3    b = −2

Interpret and Compare Lines

9. In y = 2x + 4, what is the slope and what does it tell you about the direction of the line?

Slope 2 — positive, so the line rises left to right, going up 2 for every 1 across.

10. In y = −12x + 3, how does the slope compare to a line with m = 2?

It falls instead of rising, and it is less steep — 12 is smaller than 2.

11. Compare y = 3x + 1 and y = 3x − 4.

Alike:

Same slope 3, so they are parallel.

Different:

Different y-intercepts: 1 and −4.

12. Compare y = 2x and y = −2x. How does the sign of the slope affect each line?

Same steepness, opposite directions — one rises, one falls. They are mirror images across the y-axis.

13. Which is steeper, y = 12x + 1 or y = 4x − 3? Explain how you know.

y = 4x − 3 — |4| is bigger than |12|, so it climbs faster.

Challenge

14. Perpendicular lines have slopes that multiply to −1, so m⊥ = −1m. The line y = 3x − 1 has slope 3. A second line is perpendicular to it and crosses the y-axis at (0, 9). Where do the two lines intersect?

Perpendicular slope: −13

Its equation: y = −13x + 9

Intersection: (3, 8)

15. Parallel lines have the same slope. Find the y-intercept of a line through (n, m) that is parallel to the line through (m, 3) and (4, n).

Slope through (m, 3) and (4, n): n − 34 − m

Through (n, m): y − m = n − 34 − m(x − n)

At x = 0 the intercept is b = m − n(n − 3)4 − m
