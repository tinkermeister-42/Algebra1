4.6.1Solving Problems with Linear Models

Every linear model has two pieces.

The slope (m) tells how fast something changes.

The y-intercept (b) tells where it starts.

EX 1. Streaming. $2 per episode rented plus a $7.99 monthly fee.

Let e = the number of episodes rented and P(e) = the total cost for the month

m = 2 because each episode adds $2    b = 7.99 because the fee is charged even with 0 episodes

Model: P(e) = 2e + 7.99    Cost for 10 episodes: $27.99

EX 2. Cooling soup. Soup cools from 90°C to 60°C in 10 minutes at a constant rate.

m = −3    b = 90

Model: T(t) = −3t + 90    Temperature after 15 minutes: 45°C

How much should I graph?
A model only makes sense for certain values. For the soup, times before 0 would mean before the soup was made, and after about 20 minutes the soup would be colder than the room, which cannot happen. Graph only the part that fits the situation.
EX 3. Taxi. $3 to start plus $2.25 per mile.

Model: C(d) = 2.25d + 3    Cost of a 20-mile ride: $48

EX 4. Comparing services. A ride-share charges no fee but $3.75 per mile. When is the taxi from EX 3 the better deal?

Ride-share model: R(d) = 3.75d

The lines cross near 2 miles.

Break-even cost: $7.50

For trips longer than that, the taxi is cheaper.

EX 5. Babysitting. Estrella charges an hourly rate plus a minimum fee. She charged $70 for 5 hours and $46 for 3 hours.

Why can't we just divide money by hours? there is a flat minimum fee on top of the hourly rate, so the total is not proportional to the hours

Rate (slope): m = 70 − 465 − 3 = $12 per hour

Minimum fee: substitute a point and solve for b: 46 = 12(3) + b, so b = 10

Model: C(h) = 12h + 10    Charge for 12 hours: $154

4.6.2Seeing Patterns in Data

Real data rarely lands in a perfect line, but it often shows a trend. When that pattern looks roughly straight we draw a best-fit line.

Positive correlation: as x goes up, y goes up

Negative correlation: as x goes up, y goes down

No correlation: no clear pattern

The closer the points are to the line, the stronger the correlation.
EX 6. Study time vs. test score. The best-fit line is G(h) = 4.5h + 49.5.

What does the slope 4.5 mean? each extra hour of study is worth about 4.5 more points

What does the intercept 49.5 mean? the predicted score with no studying at all

Predicted score after 7 hours of study: 81

EX 7. Car weight vs. fuel economy. The best-fit line is F(w) = −0.007w + 70.

What does the negative slope mean? heavier cars get fewer mpg - about 0.007 mpg less per pound

Does the intercept describe a real car? no Why? a car cannot weigh 0 lb

Predicted mpg for a 3450 lb car: 45.85 mpg

6. C(x) = 40x + 200 models the cost of making x items.

The 40 means: each item costs $40 to make

The 200 means: a fixed $200 of start-up cost, even for zero items

7. T(d) = 85 − 2d models temperature at d thousand feet.

Slope: the temperature drops 2° for every thousand feet of altitude

Intercept: 85° at sea level

Slope: $10.50 for each hour walked

Intercept: a $35 flat fee no matter how long the walk

Compare Two Models

9. One gym charges $30 per month plus $5 per visit. Another has no monthly fee but $9 per visit. After how many visits is the cost the same?

30 + 5v = 9v  →  30 = 4v

v = 7.5 visits - so at 8 visits the flat-fee gym pulls ahead.

10. Company A charges $12 per poster. Company B charges an $80 setup fee plus $8 per poster. At how many posters is the cost equal?

12p = 80 + 8p  →  4p = 80

p = 20 posters

11. One streaming plan is $6.50 per month plus $1 per episode. Another is $12 per month plus $0.50 per episode. Which is cheaper for 15 episodes?

6.50 + 1(15) = $21.50    12 + 0.50(15) = $19.50

The $12/month plan is cheaper for 15 episodes.

b. A real 3600 lb car gets 51.5 mpg. Residual (actual − predicted) = +6.7, so the point is above the line.
