Unit 4 - Intro to Correlation

When we collect data in the real world, it rarely lines up perfectly, but patterns still appear.

This tool lets you explore how correlation describes those patterns.

Move the slider to change the strength and direction of the correlation.

You’ll see:

  • positive correlation makes points rise together
  • negative correlation makes them fall
  • no correlation means there’s no clear pattern at all

The orange line shows the best-fit line, which summarizes the trend.
Notice how the points stay close to the line when the relationship is strong
and spread out when the relationship is weak.

To learn more, take a look at 4.6 – Linear Models in the Real World
