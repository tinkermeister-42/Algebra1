Slope-Intercept Form Investigation

Sometimes the best way to learn math is to play around with it.

Try moving the sliders in the interactive chart below. Watch what happens as you change \(m\) (slope) and \(b\) (y-intercept).

As you explore, think about these questions:

  1. What happens when you increase the slope from zero?
  2. What happens when you decrease the slope from zero?
  3. How does the line move when you change the y-intercept \(b\)?
  4. How would you describe two lines that have the same slope but different y-intercepts?
  5. Could you make a vertical line with a tool like this? Why or why not?

To learn more about slope-intercept form, take a look at 4.3 - The Equation of a Line
