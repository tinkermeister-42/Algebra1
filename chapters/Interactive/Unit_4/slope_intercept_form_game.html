Line Matching Game

In this game, you’ll practice writing equations for lines in slope–intercept form (\(y = mx + b\)).

Your goal is to match the target line that appears on the graph.

How to play:

  1. Click Start to begin the round. A green target line will appear.
  2. Type a slope (\(m\)) and y-intercept (\(b\)) for your line, then click Apply.
  3. You have 6 tries and 1 minute to match the target line exactly.
  4. Each correct match earns points — faster answers give a time bonus, and every 100 points adds an extra life.
  5. Run out of moves or time, and you’ll lose a life. Lose all lives, and the game is over.

This game helps you visualize how slope and intercept affect a line and builds intuition for graphing equations.

To learn more, take a look at 4.5 – Linear Models in the Real World
