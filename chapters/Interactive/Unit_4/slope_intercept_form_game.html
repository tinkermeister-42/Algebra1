Line Matching Game

A quick way to build intuition for how slope and intercept shape a line.

Match the hidden target line using slope–intercept form (\(y = mx + b\)).

How to play:

  1. Hit Start to begin a round.
  2. Enter your \(m\) and \(b\), then press Apply to reveal your guess.
  3. You have limited moves, and (if enabled) a timer — faster matches earn bigger multipliers.
  4. Correct answers give points; every 100 points = +1 life.
  5. Run out of moves or lives and the game ends.

To learn more, take a look at 4.6 – Linear Models in the Real World
