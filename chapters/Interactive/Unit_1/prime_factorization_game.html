Unit 1 - Prime Factorization Game

Sometimes the best way to understand a number is to take it apart.

In this game, you’ll break numbers down step by step using a factor tree. Your goal is to end with only prime numbers and correctly identify them.

How the game works

  • Click a number to split it into two factors.
  • Right-click a number to mark it as prime.
  • A number marked with P means it’s been identified as prime.
  • A number marked with C means it’s composite and has already been expanded.
  • Click the gear to investigate other options
    • Hard mode
    • Include variables (Note: use “^” character for exponents)
    • Themes

Build the tree until every branch ends in primes. Each correct move builds your streak, while mistakes reset it.

As you play, think about questions like these:

  1. How can you tell whether a number should be expanded or marked prime?
  2. Does the order you choose factors matter for the final prime factorization?
  3. What mistakes are easy to make when factoring larger numbers?
  4. How do prime numbers behave differently from composite numbers in the tree?
  5. What patterns do you notice when numbers have repeated prime factors?

Try to factor each number as cleanly and efficiently as you can.

To learn more about prime factorization and why it matters, see 1.2 – Factors, Multiples & Prime Factorization
