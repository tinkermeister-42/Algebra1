Unit 6 - Vertex Form Explorer

This widget lets you play around with quadratics in vertex form.

\[ f(x) = a(x - h)^2 + k \]

This gives a parabola with vertex \((h, k)\) and scale factor \(a\).

To learn more, take a look at 6.2 - Graphing Quadratics
