Unit 3 - Literal Equations Practice

This page gives you extra practice with 3.7 - Solving Literal Equations.

There are four sections. Each section starts with one example that is worked out for you. Then you try the problems. The answers are at the end of each section.

Your answer may look different from the answer key and still be correct. If you are not sure, check it with numbers.

Section 1 - Multiply or Divide

These formulas need only one step.

Example: Density

The density of an object is \(D = \dfrac{m}{V}\), where \(m\) is the mass and \(V\) is the volume. Solve for the mass \(m\).

\(m\) is divided by \(V\). To undo division, multiply both sides by \(V\).

\[ \begin{aligned} D &= \frac{m}{V} \\ \\ D \textcolor{red}{\times V} &= \frac{m}{V} \textcolor{red}{\times V} \sntimes{V} \\ DV &= m \end{aligned} \]

So \(m = DV\).

Check with numbers. Pick \(m = 20\) and \(V = 4\). Then \(D = \dfrac{20}{4} = 5\). Our answer says \(m = DV = 5 \times 4 = 20\). That is the mass we started with, so the answer works.

Solve each formula for the indicated variable.

  1. \(W = Fd\); solve for \(F\)

  2. \(P = IV\); solve for \(V\)

  3. \(C = \pi d\); solve for \(d\)

  4. \(V = lwh\); solve for \(w\)

  5. \(a = \frac{v}{t}\); solve for \(v\)

  6. \(\frac{2}{3}x = y\); solve for \(x\)

  7. \(ax = c\); solve for \(x\)

  8. \(5x = 2y\); solve for \(x\)

  9. \(-4y = 3x\); solve for \(y\)

  10. \(\frac{x}{k} = y\); solve for \(x\)

  1. \(F = \frac{W}{d}\)

  2. \(V = \frac{P}{I}\)

  3. \(d = \frac{C}{\pi}\)

  4. \(w = \frac{V}{lh}\)

  5. \(v = at\)

  6. \(x = \frac{3y}{2}\)

  7. \(x = \frac{c}{a}\)

  8. \(x = \frac{2y}{5}\)

  9. \(y = -\frac{3x}{4}\)

  10. \(x = ky\)

Section 2 - Add or Subtract First

These formulas need two steps. Move the terms you do not want with addition or subtraction. Then multiply or divide.

Example: From standard form

Solve \(3x + 2y = 12\) for \(y\).

\[ \begin{aligned} 3x + 2y &= 12 \\ \\ 3x + 2y \textcolor{red}{-3x} &= 12 \textcolor{red}{-3x} \snminus{3x} \\ 2y &= 12 - 3x \\ \\ 2y \textcolor{red}{\div 2} &= (12 - 3x) \textcolor{red}{\div 2} \sndiv{2} \\ y &= \frac{12 - 3x}{2} \end{aligned} \]

So \(y = \dfrac{12 - 3x}{2}\).

You can also split the fraction. Divide each term by 2.

\[ y = \frac{12}{2} - \frac{3x}{2} = 6 - \frac{3}{2}x \]

Both answers are correct. They are equivalent expressions.

When you divide \(12 - 3x\) by 2, both terms are divided by 2.

\(\dfrac{12 - 3x}{2}\) is not the same as \(6 - 3x\).

Test it with \(x = 2\). The first one gives \(\dfrac{12 - 6}{2} = 3\). The second one gives \(6 - 6 = 0\). They are different, so \(6 - 3x\) is wrong.

Solve each formula for the indicated variable.

  1. \(P = a + b + c\); solve for \(b\)

  2. \(y = 4x - k\); solve for \(k\)

  3. \(2x + y = 9\); solve for \(y\)

  4. \(x + 3y = 6\); solve for \(y\)

  5. \(4x - 2y = 10\); solve for \(y\)

  6. \(ax - b = c\); solve for \(x\)

  7. \(v = u + at\); solve for \(t\)

  8. \(S = 180n - 360\); solve for \(n\)

  9. \(8 - 2x = y\); solve for \(x\)

  10. \(mx + ny = p\); solve for \(y\)

  1. \(b = P - a - c\)

  2. \(k = 4x - y\)

  3. \(y = 9 - 2x\)

  4. \(y = \frac{6 - x}{3}\), which is the same as \(y = 2 - \frac{1}{3}x\)

  5. \(y = 2x - 5\)

  6. \(x = \frac{c + b}{a}\)

  7. \(t = \frac{v - u}{a}\)

  8. \(n = \frac{S + 360}{180}\)

  9. \(x = \frac{8 - y}{2}\)

  10. \(y = \frac{p - mx}{n}\)

Section 3 - Multi-Step Formulas

These formulas have fractions, parentheses, or more than two steps.

Example: Undo in reverse order

Solve \(y = \dfrac{3x - a}{4}\) for \(x\).

First, look at what happens to \(x\). To get \(x\) back, undo each step in the reverse order.

What happens to \(x\) How to undo it
1. Multiply by 3 3. Divide by 3
2. Subtract \(a\) 2. Add \(a\)
3. Divide by 4 1. Multiply by 4

\[ \begin{aligned} y &= \frac{3x - a}{4} \\ \\ y \textcolor{red}{\times 4} &= \frac{3x - a}{4} \textcolor{red}{\times 4} \sntimes{4} \\ 4y &= 3x - a \\ \\ 4y \textcolor{red}{+a} &= 3x - a \textcolor{red}{+a} \snplus{a} \\ 4y + a &= 3x \\ \\ (4y + a) \textcolor{red}{\div 3} &= 3x \textcolor{red}{\div 3} \sndiv{3} \\ \frac{4y + a}{3} &= x \end{aligned} \]

So \(x = \dfrac{4y + a}{3}\).

Solve each formula for the indicated variable.

  1. \(\frac{x + a}{b} = c\); solve for \(x\)

  2. \(t = \frac{d}{r}\); solve for \(r\)

  3. \(a(x + b) = c\); solve for \(x\)

  4. \(3(y - 2) = x\); solve for \(y\)

  5. \(E = \frac{1}{2}mv^2\); solve for \(m\)

  6. \(F = \frac{9}{5}C + 32\); solve for \(C\)

  7. \(y - 3 = m(x + 2)\); solve for \(x\)

  8. \(\frac{2}{3}x - a = b\); solve for \(x\)

  9. \(S = 2\pi r^2 + 2\pi rh\); solve for \(h\)

  10. \(\frac{1}{2}(x + y) = 3x - k\); solve for \(x\)

  1. \(x = bc - a\)

  2. \(r = \frac{d}{t}\)

  3. \(x = \frac{c}{a} - b\), which is the same as \(x = \frac{c - ab}{a}\)

  4. \(y = \frac{x}{3} + 2\), which is the same as \(y = \frac{x + 6}{3}\)

  5. \(m = \frac{2E}{v^2}\)

  6. \(C = \frac{5}{9}(F - 32)\)

  7. \(x = \frac{y - 3}{m} - 2\)

  8. \(x = \frac{3(a + b)}{2}\)

  9. \(h = \frac{S - 2\pi r^2}{2\pi r}\)

  10. \(x = \frac{y + 2k}{5}\)

Section 4 - Test-Style Questions

Tests often ask about literal equations with different words. The math is the same.

Example: “Which equation is equivalent?”

Which equation is equivalent to \(2x - 5y = 10\)?

  1. \(y = \dfrac{2}{5}x - 2\)
  2. \(y = -\dfrac{2}{5}x + 2\)
  3. \(y = \dfrac{2}{5}x + 10\)
  4. \(y = -2x - 2\)

Equivalent means the same equation written a different way. Every choice has \(y\) by itself. So the question is asking you to solve for \(y\).

\[ \begin{aligned} 2x - 5y &= 10 \\ \\ 2x - 5y \textcolor{red}{-2x} &= 10 \textcolor{red}{-2x} \snminus{2x} \\ -5y &= 10 - 2x \\ \\ -5y \textcolor{red}{\div (-5)} &= (10 - 2x) \textcolor{red}{\div (-5)} \sndiv{(-5)} \\ y &= -2 + \frac{2}{5}x \end{aligned} \]

So \(y = \frac{2}{5}x - 2\). The answer is a.

The wrong choices come from common mistakes. Choice b divides by \(5\) instead of \(-5\). Choice c divides only the \(x\) term. Choice d divides only the 10.

  1. The volume of a pyramid is \(V = \frac{1}{3}Bh\). Which equation can be used to find the height \(h\)?

    1. \(h = \dfrac{V}{3B}\)
    2. \(h = \dfrac{3V}{B}\)
    3. \(h = 3VB\)
    4. \(h = \dfrac{B}{3V}\)

  2. Which equation is equivalent to \(6x + 3y = 9\)?

    1. \(y = 9 - 2x\)
    2. \(y = 3 - 6x\)
    3. \(y = -2x + 3\)
    4. \(y = 2x + 3\)

  3. The equation \(ax + by = c\) is solved for \(y\). Which expression is equal to \(y\)?

    1. \(\dfrac{c - ax}{b}\)
    2. \(\dfrac{c + ax}{b}\)
    3. \(c - \dfrac{ax}{b}\)
    4. \(\dfrac{c}{b} - ax\)

  4. A car rental costs \(\$25\) plus \(\$0.50\) for each mile you drive. The total cost is \(C = 25 + 0.5m\), where \(m\) is the number of miles.

    1. Solve the formula for \(m\).
    2. You have \(\$60\). How many miles can you drive?

  5. Ana solved \(4x + 8y = 12\) for \(y\). Here is her work.

    \[ \begin{aligned} 4x + 8y &= 12 \\ 8y &= 12 - 4x \\ y &= 12 - \frac{4x}{8} \\ y &= 12 - \frac{1}{2}x \end{aligned} \]

    What mistake did Ana make? What is the correct answer?

  1. b. \(h = \frac{3V}{B}\)

  2. c. \(y = -2x + 3\)

  3. a. \(\frac{c - ax}{b}\)

    1. \(m = \frac{C - 25}{0.5}\), which is the same as \(m = 2C - 50\)

    2. \(m = 2(60) - 50 = 70\) miles

  5. Ana divided only \(4x\) by 8. She needed to divide every term by 8. The correct answer is \(y = \frac{12 - 4x}{8}\), which is the same as \(y = \frac{3}{2} - \frac{1}{2}x\).
