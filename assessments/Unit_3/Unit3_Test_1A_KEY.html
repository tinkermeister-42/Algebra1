  3. Word Problem: After paying a $4\$4 delivery fee, the total for three identical notebooks was $19\$19. Write and solve an equation to find the price of each notebook.

Answer Key

  1. x=23x = 23
  2. y=3y = 3
  3. 3n+4=193n + 4 = 19, so n=5n = 5: each notebook costs $5\$5.
  4. x=4x = 4
  5. p=7p = 7
  6. r=4r = 4
  7. m=10m = 10
  8. No solution. Both sides simplify to 4t4t, leaving −7=2-7 = 2, which is never true.
  9. x>6x > 6 — open circle at 66, shaded to the right.
  10. z≥−3z \ge -3 — closed circle at −3-3, shaded to the right. (Dividing by −3-3 flips the inequality.)
  11. h=Vπr2h = \dfrac{V}{\pi r^2}
  12. t=P−2h3t = \dfrac{P - 2h}{3}
  13. x=2y−bax = \dfrac{2y - b}{a}
