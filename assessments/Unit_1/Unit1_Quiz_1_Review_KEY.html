11. I can solve real-world problems using the GCF

  1. A teacher has 3636 red pencils and 4848 blue pencils and wants identical packs with no leftovers. What is the greatest number of packs, and how many of each colour goes in a pack?
  2. A store has 5050 stickers and 7575 erasers to split into identical prize bags with nothing left over. How many bags, and what is in each one?
  3. Two ribbons are 8484 inches and 126126 inches long. They are cut into equal pieces as long as possible, with no ribbon wasted. How long is each piece, and how many pieces come from each ribbon?
  1. 1212 packs (the GCF of 3636 and 4848); 33 red and 44 blue in each.   b. 2525 bags; 22 stickers and 33 erasers in each.   c. 4242 inches (the GCF of 8484 and 126126); 22 pieces from the shorter ribbon and 33 from the longer.
