11. I avoid common mistakes with negatives and exponents

True or False? Explain your reasoning clearly. “(−3)2=−9(-3)^2 = -9”

False. The parentheses mean the negative is squared too: (−3)2=(−3)(−3)=9(-3)^2 = (-3)(-3) = 9. Only −32-3^2 equals −9-9.
