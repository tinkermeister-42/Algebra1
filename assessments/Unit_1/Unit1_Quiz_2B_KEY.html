11. I avoid common mistakes with negatives and exponents

True or False? Explain your reasoning clearly. “(−4)2=−16(-4)^2 = -16”

False. The parentheses mean the negative is squared too: (−4)2=16(-4)^2 = 16. Only −42-4^2 equals −16-16.
